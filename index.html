Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Solar panels

How long does a solar installation take?

Most installations take 2 to 5 days, depending on the size of the array and roof complexity.

Do I need planning permission?

Solar panels usually fall under permitted development. Exceptions can apply to listed buildings and homes in conservation areas, so check the rules that apply to your property.

Do solar panels work on cloudy days?

Yes. They still generate electricity in cloudy weather, though output is reduced. A well-designed system can generate useful power throughout the year.

How much could solar reduce my energy bills?

Some homes may see reductions of around 40% to 70%, especially when paired with battery storage. Actual savings vary with system size, energy use and tariffs.

Air source heat pumps

Will a heat pump work in cold weather?

Modern heat pumps can operate in cold weather, including temperatures around -15°C. Performance depends on the model, system design and installation.

Do I need underfloor heating?

Not always. Heat pumps can work with radiators, underfloor heating or a combination of both.

Are heat pumps expensive to run?

They can cost less to run than gas or direct electric heating, especially in a well-insulated home. Actual costs depend on the system, home and energy tariffs.

EV chargers

How long does EV charger installation take?

Most installations take 1 to 2 days, depending on the consumer unit and cable routing.

Do I need Wi-Fi for a smart charger?

Smart chargers use Wi-Fi or 4G for monitoring and connected charging features.

Are chargers compatible with all electric vehicles?

We install universal chargers designed for electric and plug-in hybrid vehicles. We can confirm compatibility for your vehicle and preferred charger.

External wall insulation

How much heat loss can insulation reduce?

External wall insulation can reduce heat loss by up to 45%, helping improve comfort and reduce heating bills. Results vary by property.

Will it change the appearance of my home?

You can choose from a range of colours, textures and finishes.

How long does installation take?

Most homes take 5 to 10 days, depending on property size and weather conditions.

Does it help with damp?

It can help protect walls from penetrating damp and reduce internal condensation when the system is correctly specified and installed.
